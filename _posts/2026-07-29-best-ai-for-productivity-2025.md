AI for productivity refers to the use of artificial intelligence technologies to enhance and automate various tasks, workflows, and processes, aiming to increase efficiency, reduce time consumption, and improve overall productivity. ### Task Automation
AI-powered task automation allows users to define and automate repetitive tasks, freeing up time for more strategic and creative work. This feature is particularly useful for tasks that follow a set pattern or routine.

### Intelligent Assistance
Some AI tools offer intelligent assistance, providing users with real-time suggestions, recommendations, and insights to help them make informed decisions and stay on track.

Natural Language Processing (NLP) capabilities enable AI tools to understand and interpret human language, allowing for more natural and intuitive interactions with the tool.
