## What Is Hemingway Editor?
Hemingway Editor is a simple, web-based writing tool designed to help writers simplify their writing and make it more concise. It analyzes text and highlights complex sentences, adverbs, and passive voice, providing suggestions for improvement. The editor is named after Ernest Hemingway, known for his straightforward and simple writing style.

## Key Features Worth Knowing
### Simplified Writing
Hemingway Editor helps writers simplify their writing by highlighting complex sentences and suggesting alternative phrases.

### Adverb Detection
The tool detects adverbs in the text and recommends replacing them with more descriptive verbs.

### Passive Voice Detection
Hemingway Editor identifies instances of passive voice and suggests rephrasing them in active voice.
